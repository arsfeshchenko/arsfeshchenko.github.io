SupportPrivacy Policy

Short version: Memorasana collects nothing. It has no accounts, no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and it works fully offline.

What the app collects

None. The app does not collect, store or send any personal data, usage data, identifiers or location. Your exam answers stay on your device for the length of a round and are not saved.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs, analytics or advertising services, and it makes no network requests.

Children

Because no data is collected, none is collected from children.

If this ever changes, this page will be updated before the app does, with a new date below.
