Memorasana is an app for learning yoga pose names, operated by Arsenii Feshchenko. This policy describes information handled by the app, including its optional subscriptions and analytics.

Analytics

Memorasana uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the app is used and improve it. Google Analytics automatically collects an app instance identifier, app and device information, approximate region derived from a masked IP address, and app activity such as sessions and screen views. It can also measure in-app purchase and subscription events, including product identifiers and prices. Analytics is not used by Memorasana to show ads or for advertising personalization.

Google processes this information to provide analytics. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase and Google's Privacy Policy.

Subscriptions and payments

Apple processes subscription purchases and payments through the App Store. Memorasana uses Apple's StoreKit to check subscription access and restore purchases. We do not receive or store your full payment card details. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

Feedback and information on your device

If you choose to email feedback, we receive your email address and the information you include so we can respond. Your exam answers are used on your device during the exam. The app also stores preferences on your device, such as whether a feedback prompt has been shown.

Retention and your choices

Analytics and feedback are retained only as needed for their purposes, subject to the settings and retention periods of the services used and applicable law. Contact us with questions or requests concerning your information. You can manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Changes

We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version and effective date are shown here.
