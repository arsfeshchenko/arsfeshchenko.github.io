SupportPrivacy Policy

Memorasana teaches the Sanskrit names of yoga poses. Each name is drawn as what it means: Bakāsana is a crane, so a crane holds the pose.

Contact

Questions, a wrong translation, or a pose you'd like added? Write to ars.feshchenko@gmail.com. I read every message and usually reply within a few days.

Common questions

Does it need the internet?
No. Everything is inside the app and works offline.

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-in, no ads and no tracking.

Which languages?
English, Ukrainian and Spanish. The app follows your iPhone's language.

How does the exam work?
Tap 🎓 Exam: you get ten random poses and pick the right Sanskrit name out of four.

Is this medical advice?
No. Memorasana teaches names. With an injury or a health condition, check with a teacher or doctor before you practise.
